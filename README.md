### 🚀 Export Roblox assets to your favorite animation tools in just one click!

Riglify is a modern, community-focused platform built for creators. We make it easy to export Roblox avatars, bundles, models, and accessories directly into **Prisma3D** and other major animation software.

We built Riglify to fill the gap left by outdated, broken, or unsafe tools, keeping mobile-friendly accessibility and creator freedom at the heart of everything we do. Inspired by the legacy of *faizda.my.id*, we are here to stay.
